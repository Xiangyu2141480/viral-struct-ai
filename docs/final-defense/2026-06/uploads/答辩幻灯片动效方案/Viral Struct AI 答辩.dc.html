STRUCTURE DEFINITION
结构 = role × subtype × motionToken × packaging
ShotSlot.ts 
type ShotSlot = {
  role:        "hook" | "selling_point" | "cta";
  subtype:     "kinetic_reveal" | "assembly";
  motionToken: "chaos_to_order" | "cta_reveal";
  packaging:   "subtitle" | "card";
};
role — 这一段在「干什么」
subtype — 结构怎么走
motionToken — 运动语法
packaging — 画面包装
结构协议,让「感觉」变成可计算对象。
